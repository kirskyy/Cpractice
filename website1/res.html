Research and Innovation

Driving Knowledge and Societal Impact

Mount Kenya University is a hub of vibrant research and innovation, committed to generating new knowledge, solving real-world challenges, and contributing to national and global development agendas.

Our research endeavors are guided by a commitment to excellence, integrity, and relevance, fostering a culture of inquiry among faculty, staff, and students. We believe research is key to unlocking potential and driving progress.

Our Focus

Key Research Areas

MKU's research initiatives span a broad spectrum of disciplines, addressing critical issues relevant to East Africa and beyond.

Health & Medical Sciences

Focusing on public health, tropical diseases, non-communicable diseases, and pharmaceutical research.

Agriculture & Food Security

Innovating in sustainable agriculture, crop improvement, food processing, and climate-smart farming.

ICT & Digital Innovations

Pioneering research in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, big data analytics, and digital transformation.

Education & Social Development

Exploring pedagogical innovations, community development, governance, and peace studies.

Business & Economic Development

Investigating entrepreneurship, financial markets, sustainable business practices, and economic policy.

Environmental Science & Climate Change

Conducting research on environmental conservation, renewable energy, and climate change adaptation.

Our researchers regularly publish in reputable journals and present at international conferences.
