About

Welcome to Mount Kenya University

Mount Kenya University (MKU) is a Chartered University committed to a broad-based, holistic and inclusive system of education based in Kenya.

The University has a Board of Trustees which under the stewardship of Prof. Simon N. Gicharu has investment in science, technology, teaching and research infrastructure and a conducive environment for producing graduates who are generators of new knowledge, innovators, qualified employees and job creators.
