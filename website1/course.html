Admissions to Mount Kenya University

Begin Your Journey at MKU!

Mount Kenya University (MKU) welcomes you to explore a world of academic excellence and opportunity. We are dedicated to providing holistic education and training that prepares you for a successful future. Our admission process is designed to be straightforward and accessible.

Whether you're looking to pursue a Certificate, Diploma, Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctorate degree, MKU offers a diverse range of programs across various schools and departments.

Admissions Process

How to Apply

1. Choose Your Program

Explore our wide range of academic programs, including Certificates, Diplomas, Bachelor's, Master's, and Doctoral degrees. Ensure you meet the minimum entry requirements for your desired course.

View Programs

2. Submit Your Application

You can apply online through our student portal or download and fill out the application form. Ensure all supporting documents are attached.

Apply Online Now

3. Await Admission & Register

Your application will be reviewed carefully. Upon approval, you'll receive an admission letter. Follow the instructions to register and pay your fees to secure your spot.

Key Information

General Admission Requirements

Academic Qualifications

Minimum mean grade scores achieved in High School (KCSE or equivalent), with specific grade requirements for core subjects depending on the program. Bachelor's, Diploma, and Certificate programs have varying requirements. For Postgraduate programs, relevant undergraduate degrees are required.

Supporting Documents
  • ✓ Copies of certified academic certificates and transcripts
  • ✓ One (1) recent passport-size photograph
  • ✓ Copy of National ID/Passport/Birth Certificate
  • ✓ Official translation of academic records (if language of study was not English)
Application Fees (Non-refundable)
  • East African Applicants:
    • Ksh. 1,000 (Certificates, Diplomas & Undergraduate programs)
    • Ksh. 2,000 (Post-Graduate programs)
  • Non-East African Applicants:
    • US$ 50 (Certificates, Diplomas & Undergraduate programs)
    • US$ 100 (Post-Graduate programs)

Payment via bank deposit only. No cash payments.

Modes of Study & Intakes

MKU offers flexible modes of study:

  • Regular (Full-time)
  • Part-time (Evening/Weekends)
  • Open, Distance and Electronic Learning (ODeL) / Digital Varsity

Common intakes are in January, May, and September. Specific program intakes may vary.

For detailed and program-specific requirements, please refer to the MKU Academic Programmes page or download the official prospectus.

Frequently Asked Questions

Admission is often on a first-come, first-served basis. While there are no strict deadlines, we encourage early application, especially for popular programs. Check the academic calendar for registration cut-off dates for each intake.

It is generally recommended to apply for one program at a time. If you wish to change your program after admission, you can apply for a transfer during the registration period, subject to approval and meeting the new program's requirements.

MKU students are eligible for Higher Education Loans Board (HELB) loans and bursaries. We strongly recommend applying for these. Information on other potential financial aid options may be available through the university's finance department.
